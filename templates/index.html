Key System Features

Engineered for academic evaluation and real-world retail data analytics.

📤

1. Smart CSV Ingestion

Upload retail sales CSV files with flexible column alias mapping (`order_id`, `product`, `category`, `price`, `quantity`, `total_sales`).

🛡️

2. Automated Data Validation

Detects missing fields, unparseable dates, negative values, exact duplicate rows, and discrepancies in Total Sales formulas before saving.

🧹

3. Transparent Data Cleaning

Sanitizes records, standardizes dates to `YYYY-MM-DD`, recalculates missing `Total_Sales` (`Quantity × Price`), and outputs a transparent change audit.

📊

4. Real-time KPI Dashboard

Instantly computes Total Revenue, Total Orders, Units Sold, Average Order Value (AOV), Best-Selling Products, and Highest-Revenue Months.

📈

5. Interactive Visualizations

Powered by Chart.js. Explore monthly sales curves, product revenues, category distribution pie charts, and chronological trends.

🗄️

6. MySQL Persistent Storage

Persists sanitized records to normalized relational tables (`sales`, `products`) with parameterized queries, duplicate prevention, and search.
